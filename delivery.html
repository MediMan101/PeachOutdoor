Delivery

Tractor and Equipment Delivery from Clanton, Alabama

Peach Outdoor sells tractors, mowers and equipment to customers well beyond Clanton. We arrange delivery through freight carriers, typically within about 500 miles of our Clanton, Alabama lot, and we have delivered to customers as far away as upstate New York.

Free freight on select units

On some units, freight is free to the customer. Which ones changes with the equipment and current offers, so ask us when you call and we will tell you whether freight is included on the unit you want. When it is not included, we quote the freight up front so there are no surprises.

Where we deliver

Most of our out-of-state deliveries go to the states below. Pick yours to see tractors in stock and delivery details. We also deliver to Florida and South Carolina, and beyond. If you are farther out, call us. We have done it before.

How it works

Pick your tractor, mower or implement, then call or message us. We will confirm the unit and quote freight to your address, and we hand it off to a freight carrier. Financing can be started online before you decide.

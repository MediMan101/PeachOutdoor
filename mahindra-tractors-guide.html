Why buy a Mahindra

Mahindra markets its tractors as the “Toughest Tractors On Earth,” and the way they are built backs that up: heavy cast iron, made to last and made to work. Their motto, “Push More, Pull More, Lift More,” is right on the mark. Mahindras carry high lift capacity on both the loader and the three-point hitch. Other brands may run fast, but Mahindra tractors are all about the work.

  • No diesel particulate filter or DEFMahindra's mCRD engines are designed to clean up exhaust without a diesel particulate filter (DPF) or diesel exhaust fluid (DEF). That means no filter burn-off cycles, no DEF to buy and refill, and less downtime in the middle of a job.
  • Heavy cast-iron constructionBuilt heavier than many tractors in their class. The extra weight keeps the tires planted for traction and steadier handling on slopes, wet ground and rough pasture.
  • Strong loader lift capacityStrong hydraulics and a sturdy loader subframe handle round bales, full buckets and heavy pallets, which matters most if the loader is the attachment you use the most.
  • 7-year powertrain warrantyMahindra backs the engine and powertrain with a long-term warranty (up to 7 years or 3,000 hours, with no deductible, per Mahindra). Ask us for the exact coverage terms on the tractor you're looking at.
  • Buying from Peach OutdoorBuying a Mahindra tractor from Peach Outdoor in Clanton, Alabama combines heavy-duty, cast-iron Mahindra equipment with local dealership support: an in-house service and parts shop, flexible financing options, and a 7-year limited powertrain warranty. Founded in 2014 by owners Mark and John, our team brings hands-on, real-world product knowledge to Central Alabama landowners and farmers.
